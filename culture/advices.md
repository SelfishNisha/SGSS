# Screen sharing culture

## Screen sharing advices

1. Try to perform the screenshare as quickly as possible, speed is important because it gives you more time to check additional things.
> Obviously, take your time to check things properly, but always try to improve your speed.

2. If you don't have everything you want or need to check during a screenshare in mind, or you simply forget things that you should check regularly, a good practice would be to create a notepad or a personal guide that you keep open throughout the screenshare.

3. The more you can automate using programs, the better your reviews will become. Automating processes with programs is a good way to ensure that you check everything accurately.

4. When you find something that already provides sufficient grounds for a ban, stop the screenshare and ban the cheater. If you continue using your methods, they will learn more about how you conduct screenshares and may therefore be able to bypass your methods in the future.

5. A good practice, if both you and the user have enough time, is to start with your basic methods and, if you still don't find anything, move on to more advanced methods. The ideal scenario is to find a cheat without revealing your best cheat-detection methods.

6. To improve your screensharing skills against bypasses, you can practice on your own computer or a VM. Try to hide a cheat as effectively as possible, then try to find it. After that, try hiding it even better and attempt to find it again.
