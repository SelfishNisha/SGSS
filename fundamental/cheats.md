# Screen sharing fundamentals

## Cheats

A cheat is any illegal modification or unfair advantage in a game. Cheats can include external programs, autoclickers, macros, advantageous mods (X-ray, AutoTotem, Reach, hacked clients), illegal texture packs (overlays, anti-invisibility, X-ray, etc.), ghost clients, and hacked clients.

There are clearly several types of cheats, and each one works differently. However, in general, cheats are hacks or unfair advantages, and cheaters are the people who use them.
