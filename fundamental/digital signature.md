# Screen sharing fundamentals

## Digital signature

Digital signatures are extremely useful during screenshares (SS) when verifying whether a file is actually what it claims to be. They act as a form of identification that developers attach to their applications to verify that they are official and authentic.

For example, Discord, Spotify, and Everything are all digitally signed by their respective developers.

When a program impersonates an official application but is not actually legitimate, or when it has been cracked, it may not have a valid digital signature.

This does not mean that every program without a digital signature is a cheat or crack. However, it can provide a stronger indication of which files are suspicious and which are not. Though keep that in mind, apps without sigs are not necessarily cheats or malware since indie devs or even hobbyists release their apps unsigned for their respective reasons.

Most cheats (with some exceptions, such as Vape, OpAutoClick, etc.) do not have digital signatures. This can be useful later when detecting various cheats, especially considering that most files that come with a PC have digital signatures.

To check whether a program has a digital signature without using external software, simply open its properties and navigate to the Digital Signatures tab.
