# Screen sharing culture

## What is screen sharing?

Screen sharing is a common practice on both competitive and non-competitive servers to prevent cheating.

## How is it done?

First, a user, usually called a "screensharer," connects to the PC of the user they suspect of cheating, and then uses methods capable of detecting whether the user has cheated.

The screensharer must use good methods to avoid being bypassed, and for this, methods related to digital forensics and cybersecurity are often used, since detecting a cheat is similar to detecting malware
